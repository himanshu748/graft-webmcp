# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and constructive; assume good intent.
- Welcome newcomers and give feedback on ideas, not people.
- Accept criticism gracefully.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or trolling.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and ban participants who violate this code. Report concerns by [opening an issue](https://github.com/himanshu748/graft-webmcp/issues/new/choose) or contacting [@himanshu748](https://github.com/himanshu748).

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
