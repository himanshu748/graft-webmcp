Plain-language glossary

Fixed viewpoint

A place you can describe and find again without adding a marker to the site.

Filtered light

Light broken by leaves, mesh or another cover, with soft patches that shift over time.

Surface water

Water visible above soil or stone. It is recorded separately from dampness.

Read a wet edge

Start from one fixed viewpoint. Look for the first continuous sign of moisture, then note any isolated patches beyond it.

Name the light

Choose one shared category before describing color or mood. Shared language makes visits comparable.

Log surface water

Record the broad condition first. Add location, shape and movement only when they help another observer return.

Trace wind shelter

Compare exposed and sheltered points at the same moment. A single moving leaf is not enough evidence.

Record without collecting

Keep feet on durable ground. Use photographs and notes instead of removing samples.

Return to a marker

Describe direction and distance from an existing landmark. Avoid paint, tape and placed objects.
